# [The New Jobs AI Will Create](https://youtube.com/watch?v=WhAAKxPlMhw)

*   **Core argument**: Growing questioning of the premise that AI agents getting better means less human work -- we should explore what types of new jobs AI will create, not just which ones are at risk.

*   **Critique of industry narrative**: The industry fails to explore what types of new jobs AI will create, focusing almost entirely on which jobs are at risk.

*   **Call to action**: First-principles analysis rather than backward-looking productivity extrapolation.
