### Episode 4: The Best Way to Talk to Your Agents (12 May)
https://youtube.com/watch?v=NNxGQw1uQHc

Anthropic weighing another round at $900B valuation. Cerebras IPO pricing upside to $150-160/share. Intel/Apple chip deal. OpenAI launches Chrome plugin for Codex with direct browser access.

The Markdown vs HTML debate for agent handoffs. Knowledge work's atomic unit has shifted from production to scaffolding/staging for agents. The "liminal space" between brainstorming and building now contains most of our time, requiring documents encoding "mixed doneness" -- what's decided, what's exploratory, what's provisional. HTML's visual hierarchy, tabs, progressive disclosure, and color coding make it better than markdown for this. Markdown for machine, HTML for human.

### Episode 5: The New Jobs AI Will Create (11 May)
https://youtube.com/watch?v=WhAAKxPlMhw

The job discussion must shift from labor supply (AI displaces) to demand expansion. Introduces six demand elasticities: price, access, complexity, continuity, personalization, and relational. These unlock new demand through "affordability unlock" (same menu, cheaper) and "possibility unlock" (completely new service models).

When AGI can do tasks, the question isn't "can AI do it?" but "does AI-only delivery satisfy the demand?" -- the "human premium" categories that won't transfer: relationship, presence, trust, accountability, translation, behavior change, and provenance.

Healthcare case study: AI enables continuous preventative care (now operationally impossible), creating net-new roles: continuous care navigators (276K-1.2M jobs), care plan outcome specialists (hundreds of thousands), and health data operations specialists (20K-40K).
