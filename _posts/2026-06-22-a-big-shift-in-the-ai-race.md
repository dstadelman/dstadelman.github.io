# Summary: A Big Shift in the AI Race

The AI Daily Brief covers two major developments reshaping the AI landscape: the ongoing fallout between the US government and Anthropic over access restrictions on Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5, and the rapidly accelerating ambitions of Elon Musk's SpaceX, which just went public at a $2.6 trillion valuation and acquired Cursor for $60 billion. Together, these stories signal a fundamental realignment in AI power dynamics.

* **Anthropic-government conflict over Mythos and Fable five days in**: US authorities imposed conditions forcing Anthropic to shut down Claude Mythos 5 and Claude Fable 5, with technical negotiations continuing in Washington. Anthropic sent Chief Compute Officer Tom Brown, Head of External Affairs Sarah Heck, Head of Red Teaming Logan Graham, and senior security researcher Nicholas Carlini to explain their position. The Wall Street Journal profiled Carlini as a respected hacker turned AI skeptic. Former Commerce Department officials reportedly said Anthropic came to every fork in the road and took the wrong fork. The ban extends beyond Fable 5 to Mytos 5, with sources saying Commerce was willing to allow Fable back if Anthropic fixed the jailbreak that triggered the ban. Over the weekend, 74 reported that the administration feared China gained access to Mytos, and the Washington Post reported expanded access to Mytos via Project Glasswing led to the crisis -- roughly 50 firms were added as recipients, including a South Korean telecom company with suspected Chinese government ties. Luta Security CEO Katie Moussouris argued that Fable's behavior -- refusing to review insecure code but generating patches to fix bugs -- was exactly how cyber-defense models should work, similar to how GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8 operate.
* **SpaceX's $2.6 trillion ascent and Cursor acquisition**: SpaceX went public with its IPO, and its stock closed at $201.80 -- up 49% from the IPO price -- valuing the company at $2.6 trillion and making Elon Musk the world's first trillionaire. [unverified] The company also announced it has acquired Cursor for $60 billion, making Cursor a wholly owned SpaceX subsidiary. [unverified] Cursor has reportedly hit a $4 billion run rate with 7x year-over-year growth, and has released Composer 2.5 at roughly a tenth the cost of Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5. [unverified] Cursor is now teasing a new model trained from scratch, the same size as Claude Opus and GPT-5.5. [unverified] Bill Ackman noted that SpaceX's ability to make "economically, strategically, and technologically accretive acquisitions" is a key component of its value.
* **Broader AI race implications**: The episode argues that mature agentic AI economics are becoming clear as companies grapple with full-agentic workload costs. OpenAI has filed confidentially for an IPO later this year, and audited financials show $13 billion in 2025 revenue against $21.2 billion in operational losses -- though OpenAI noted the majority of costs included a $30 billion non-cash accounting change linked to conversion to a public benefit company. [unverified] OpenAI reportedly generated steady profit on inference, with $13 billion in revenue against $7.5 billion in direct costs in 2025, and holds $73 billion in cash and marketable securities. [unverified] Cybersecurity experts oppose the Mythos ban via open letter with over 100 signatures. Agatha Demeray of the Council on Foreign Relations told the Financial Day that Washington has "done more to boost the appeal of Chinese AI models than Beijing ever could have hoped." [unverified] The Department of Justice intervened in a Clean Air Act lawsuit against xAI, claiming Grok is vital to national security, including supporting military operations. [unverified] Dario Amodei, Sam Altman, Demis Hassabis, and Mistral's Arthur Mensch are expected to have a 2.5-hour lunch together at the G7 summit in France. [unverified]

Ultimately, the AI race is undergoing rapid acceleration -- from SpaceX's new status as the fifth-largest company in the world to an Anthropic locked in government negotiations over its most advanced models, to OpenAI's looming IPO and financial scrutiny. The intersection of AI, geopolitics, and trillion-dollar valuations is reshaping what it means to compete in this space.
