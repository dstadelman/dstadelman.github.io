## Summary
**Headlines**
- **Cerebras IPO & mega-IPO pipeline**: Opened at $40B, briefly hit $100B, settled at 68% gains for a $66B market cap. Sets precedent for expected SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI listings. Contrarians warn of detachment from fundamentals; bulls cite infinite inference demand.
- **Figma's AI-driven recovery**: Stock up 8% post-earnings after 46% revenue growth. Introduced AI usage caps without hurting retention (75% of customers stay within or pay for the tier).
- **Nvidia's quiet surge**: +4.7% in a single session, pushing toward $6T valuation as market capital returns to AI infrastructure.
- **OpenAI vs. Apple friction**: OpenAI reportedly considering breach-of-contract action over Apple's under-delivery on Siri/ChatGPT integration. Apple internally shifting to Claude for coding and testing native Claude/Gemini system access on iPhone.
- **Anthropic's $900B round**: $30B raise at $900B valuation, nearly tripling Series G. Sequoia and Altimeter co-leading.
- **Microsoft cancels Claude Code licenses**: Terminating Anthropic's enterprise codes in June, forcing devs to GitHub Copilot CLI. Cites cost-cutting and desire to drive internal tool adoption.
- **Claude Myths security leap**: Researchers used Myths to chain exploits into macOS kernel memory. Mozilla credited Myths with finding 423 bugs in one month. UK AI Security Institute showed 60% automated attack success vs. GPT-5.5's 10%.

**Main Episode**
- **Codex mobile & the persistent agent shift**: OpenAI deployed full Codex workflows into the ChatGPT mobile app. Shifts AI from chat interface to persistent operator; humans move from execution to async triage/approval. Engineers describe laptops becoming "satellite devices" to always-on Codex instances.
- **Consumer vs. work AI divergence**: Consumer AI behaves "normally" per the "AI as normal technology" thesis, facing pushback when forced into products. Work AI remains "abnormal" — knowledge work is shifting from doing to managing synthetic fleets. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft are work-lane focused; Apple and Meta are consumer-lane focused.
- **Google's dual strategy at I/O**: Google remains the only major lab pursuing both. Expected: Gemini Spark (always-on personal agent leveraging 20+ years of user context) and Gemini 3.2 Flash (rumored ~92% of GPT-5.5 performance at 15-20x lower inference cost, sub-200ms latency). Wall Street may overlook cost efficiency for raw benchmarks, but builders will notice.
- **Google's agentic harness ambiguity**: No consolidated coding/agent platform (Gemini CLI, AI Studio, and Jules all exist without clear unification). I/O should clarify or consolidate this, or Google cedes the work-AI harness race.

**Key Takeaway**
The AI industry is entering a subsidy-ending phase where consumer/work divergence forces strategic clarity. Google's I/O position hinges on whether it can leverage cost leadership and deep personal context without abandoning its work-AI ambitions, while the broader market watches Cerebras, Figma, and Nvidia signal that the AI capital cycle is firmly restarting.
