## What Google Needs to Do at I/O This Week

Cerebras's IPO is overshadowing the I/O build-up, with its stock doubling on day one to hit a $66B market cap. This is fueling speculation about an AI IPO season, with SpaceQ, Anthropic, and OpenAI all reportedly targeting late 2026.

OpenAI extended Codex to ChatGPT mobile, enabling full agent workflows from a phone app -- a shift the host calls the move from "assisted" to "agentic" AI. Meanwhile, tensions between OpenAI and Apple surfaced, with reports suggesting OpenAI may sue Apple for breach of contract over delayed ChatGPT integration.

On the funding side, Anthropic reportedly raised $30B at a $900B valuation from Sequoia and Altimeter, which would put it ahead of OpenAI's last valuation. Microsoft, meanwhile, canceled Claude Code licenses for internal teams, shifting to GitHub Copilot CLI instead.

Claude 3 Opus's security capabilities drew attention -- researchers used it to discover a novel macOS kernel exploit, and Mozilla found 423 bugs in a_month with it, more than the previous 15 months. The UK AI Security Institute scored it at 6/10 on automated attack benchmarks versus 1/10 for GPT-5.5.

## In Defense of Tokenmaxxing

Companies including Meta, OpenAI, Disney, and Visa are incentivizing employees to consume the most AI tokens -- calling it "AI adoption" but critics call it wasteful conspicuous consumption. The host defends this as necessary experimentation: the shift from assisted to agentic AI requires burning tokens to discover best practices. Even if 100% of experiment tokens produce no immediate financial return, the learning compounds. Companies that incentivize experimentation will be "light-years ahead" in remaking their businesses for the agentic era.

## Towards AI That Can Actually Interact

OpenAI's DeployCo joint venture was formally announced -- a $4B partnership with 19 consulting/PE partners led by TPG to deploy forward engineers in enterprises, built around the acquisition of engineering firm Tomorrow. On the crypto front, both Anthropic and OpenAI declared unauthorized secondary market tokens (blockchain-backed SPV claims on their stock) as void and worthless, causing a market crash on these gray markets.

The White House walked back FDA-style regulatory proposals for AI models. Trump's China envoy notably had Jensen Huang absent, suggesting Nvidia chips may be "off the table" in trade talks while zero H200 export licenses have been approved. Meta Moritz's lab released "Interaction Models" -- a model class trained for continuous, real-time interaction running micro-turns at 200ms intervals.

## The Best Way to Talk to Your Agents

Anthropic's Tariq Shahippar's post "The Unreasonable Effectiveness of HTML" went viral (~10M views), arguing HTML is a better format for agent handoffs. The host agrees but argues for a deeper reason: our jobs are shifting from "producing a thing" to "staging work for an agent to produce." HTML serves as a richer canvas for specs, plans, and interactive handoff documents. The host counters the token cost argument -- the value is in information quality, not raw token efficiency.

Cerebras reconsidered its IPO price from $115-125 to $150-160 per share, boosting implied valuation from ~$27B to >$34B, with orders at ~20x supply. TSMC reported its slowest sales growth in 6 months at 17.5% annualized. Apple signed a preliminary chip deal with Intel to diversify from TSMC. OpenAI's Chrom plugin gives Codex direct browser access for live sessions.

## The New Jobs AI Will Create

The host argues the "job apocalypse" narrative rests on the lump of labor fallacy. AI expands demand through six types of elasticity: price, access, complexity, continuity, personalization, and relational. AI creates two unlocks -- affordability (making existing services cheaper) and possibility (making entirely new service categories operationally possible).

Seven categories of "human premium" were outlined: relationship/continuity, embodied presence, trust, accountability, translation between messy human needs and AI, behavior change, and provenance/status (human-made value). Healthcare serves as a case study -- AI could transform reactive episodic care into continuous, personalized, data-driven preventive care, creating entirely new roles and professions.
