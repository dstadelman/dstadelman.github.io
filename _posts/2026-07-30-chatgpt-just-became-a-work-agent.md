# Summary: ChatGPT Just Became a Work Agent

[AI Daily Brief - ChatGPT Just Became a Work Agent](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/nlw/episodes/ChatGPT-Just-Became-a-Work-Agent-e3lu1ln)

In this episode, the cover story explores OpenAI's July 9 launch of ChatGPT Work -- an agentic harness that extends Codex-like workflows to general knowledge work -- alongside the full release of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 model family with its three-tier Sol/Terra/Luna architecture. The headlines also cover Cursor's push into personal-assistant territory, Meta's aggressive AI infrastructure expansion including a billion-dollar Canadian data center and in-house chip production, and several corporate governance developments across the AI industry.

* **Cursor expands beyond coding**: Cursor (now part of SpaceX AI) is reportedly developing "Sand," a general-purpose agent built on Grok 4.5 aimed at standard office tasks like email and spreadsheets. Internal rollout began in June. The move signals Cursor's bid to grow beyond its software engineer base into broader knowledge work -- the same sector OpenAI is targeting with ChatGPT Work.
* **OpenAI retracts SWE Bench Pro support**: In a new audit, OpenAI found 30% of tasks on the leading coding benchmark to be broken, citing publicly available tasks contaminating training data, hidden requirements, contradictory instructions, and overly strict tests. OpenAI is formally withdrawing its endorsement, signaling a broader industry shift away from SWE Bench toward competing benchmarks each company publishes.
* **OpenAI's national security principles**: The company published new rules limiting government/military use of its technology -- explicitly ruling out mass domestic surveillance, high-stakes decisions (including over the use of force) without human judgment, and uses evading legal oversight. These align closely with Anthropic's established red lines.
* **Anthropic appoints Ben Bernanke to benefit trust**: Former Federal Reserve Chair Ben Bernanke has joined the board of Anthropic's long-term benefit trust, which gains majority voting control over Anthropic's corporate board by next year. The trust serves as an independent oversight body -- separate from shareholders -- meant to balance commercial success with public good as Anthropic develops increasingly powerful AI systems.
* **Meta's $10B Canadian data center and revived chip program**: Meta broke ground on a 1-gigawatt AI data center in Alberta with $60 million in community infrastructure contributions. The company also confirmed its in-house AI chip -- designed with Broadcom, manufactured by TSMC and Samsung -- is on track for production this September, one year after being shelved. Analysts now expect Meta to deploy 7 gigawatts of compute capacity this year and double that in 2027.
* **GPT-5.6 arrives as a three-tier family**: GPT-5.6 splits into Sol (flagship), Terra (midsize), and Luna (cost-efficient). Sol outperforms Claude Opus 4.8 on most benchmarks at significantly lower cost and matches or exceeds Fable 5 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at one-third its cost. Early adopter consensus: Sol is fast, ideal for active collaboration; Claude remains better for massive autonomous tasks.
* **ChatGPT Work launched July 9**: OpenAI's new agentic mode lets a single agent gather context across connected tools (Notion, Google Drive, Microsoft 365), run multi-step workflows on a cloud instance even when your laptop is closed, and deliver finished work -- budgets, dashboards, marketing briefs. Early testimonials report dramatic time savings, though public reception has been muted with confusion over naming and the relationship between ChatGPT Work and ChatGPT Chat.
* **Meta unveils Muse Spark 1.1**: At an unexpected announcement, Meta released a new model competitive with Opus 4.8 and GPT-5.5 on reasoning benchmarks, leading on agentic tasks like Jobbench and MCP Atlas. It's notably fast (three times faster than top competitors) and cheap -- $0.92 to test vs $5.09 for Opus 4.8 -- signaling Meta's push into the consumer-friendly personal-assistant AI space.
* **The cost/efficiency race is now the main race**: Across all major model launches this week -- GPT-5.6, Grok 4.5, Muse Spark 1.1, and even prior Cognition SWE-1.7 -- performance gains are increasingly matched with aggressive pricing pressures. xAI (SpaceX AI) and Meta are back in serious enterprise conversation, no longer just consumer players.

The overarching story of this week: the frontier performance arms race is merging with a fierce cost competition layer. OpenAI's ChatGPT Work signals that the "harness" -- the system around a model -- matters as much as the model itself. The companies delivering useful agentic workflows at low token costs will win, not just raw benchmark scores.
