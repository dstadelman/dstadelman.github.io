## The New Jobs AI Will Create (11 May 2026)
https://youtube.com/watch?v=WhAAKxPlMhw

- The episode challenged the assumption that AI only affects jobs as a labor supply story, arguing that demand elasticity is the missing variable: price, access, complexity, continuity, personalization, and relational elasticity all stretch demand as AI lowers costs
- Two "unlocks" were framed: the affordability unlock (existing services reaching new buyers at lower cost) and the possibility unlock (entirely new service models becoming viable)
- Seven categories of "human premium" — value that persists even when AGI can do the underlying tasks: relationship, embodied presence, trust, accountability, translation, behavior change, and provenance/status
- Healthcare case study proposed new roles: continuous care navigators managing 150 patients (276K potential jobs), care plan outcome specialists, and health data operations specialists (20-40K potential jobs)
- A framework for AI-era work was outlined across six role families: navigators, continuous support workers, AI-augmented service operators, data and ops specialists, QA/compliance roles, and escalation specialists
- The argument: AI creates more supply but also more demand, and the human premium ensures durable roles even in a world of increasingly capable agents
